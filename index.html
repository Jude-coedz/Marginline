THE THINKING BEHIND IT ↘

You don't need another automation.
You need a shared point of judgment.

Shopify Flow, n8n, Make and catalog imports can each follow their own rules. But independently sensible changes can produce a commercially unsafe combined outcome.

Marginline's proposed role is simple: evaluate the whole planned release against live merchant constraints before approved workflows proceed.
